# VirTech Team Directory

This repository is the reference for **who works at VirTech Systems and what they do**.
Use it to answer questions like "who handles X?", "who's on site in Y area?", or
"who should I escalate to?".

## About VirTech Systems
- Managed Service Provider (MSP), ~30 employees, based in DeLand, FL
- Clients mostly in Central Florida, plus the rest of FL and some other states
- Remote helpdesk plus onsite techs who travel to client offices
- Standard stack: FortiGate firewalls/routers and FortiAPs, Cisco switches

## Files
- `TEAM.md` — the employee roster (name, title, team, role/responsibilities, notes)
- `org-chart.html` — visual org chart built from `TEAM.md` (open in a browser)

## How to maintain
- Only record what the user tells us or what comes from an authoritative source
  (e.g., the Microsoft 365 directory). Never guess names, titles, or pronouns.
- Keep `TEAM.md` grouped by team and sorted by name within each team.
- When someone changes roles or leaves, update or remove their entry and commit.
- Keep `org-chart.html` in sync with `TEAM.md` whenever the roster changes.
